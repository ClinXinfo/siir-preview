Why Choose Us

Our interventional radiologists bring extensive training and experience from top centers in Australia, the United Kingdom, and North America. They have worked in high-volume academic hospitals and new breast screening units throughout New Zealand.

We specialize in interventional and musculoskeletal radiology, with a strong focus on accuracy and targeted care. Whatever your intervention needs, we are here to provide the support and expertise needed for successful outcomes.

Our Main Services

We provide a growing range of interventional procedures, tailored to meet the needs of both acute and elective care patients across Canterbury and the South Island.

1 Vascular Access

Tunnelled Dialysis Catheters

Long-term vascular access for patients requiring regular dialysis treatments.

Ports

Subcutaneous ports for chemotherapy administration and blood sampling.

PICCs and Midlines

Peripherally inserted central catheters for medium to long-term intravenous access.

2 Osteoarthritis Vessel Embolisation (GAE)

Knee OA Treatment

Outpatient procedure that reduces pain and inflammation in osteoarthritic knees by targeting specific blood vessels supplying synovial tissue.

3 Vascular Procedures

Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD)

Intervention, stenting, and angioplasty procedures to improve blood flow in peripheral arteries.

Uterine Fibroid Embolisation (UFE)

Minimally invasive treatment for uterine fibroids, reducing symptoms through targeted embolisation.

Varicose Vein Treatment

Treatment of varicose veins with minimally invasive techniques including laser ablation and sclerotherapy.

Pelvic Congestion Syndrome

Embolisation treatment for pelvic vein congestion causing chronic pelvic pain.

Acute & Chronic DVT Management

Managing deep venous thrombosis with thrombolysis, stenting, and prevention of recurrence.
