Our Services

Our expert team offers minimally invasive, image-guided procedures that provide rapid relief, improved outcomes, and faster recovery—all without the need for traditional surgery.

Achilles Tendon

Minimally invasive treatments for Achilles tendon injuries and conditions, promoting faster healing and recovery.

Elbow Pain

Targeted interventions to relieve elbow pain caused by various conditions, including tendinitis and bursitis.

Knee Pain

Advanced treatments for knee pain, including osteoarthritis, using image-guided injections and therapies.

Hip Pain

Minimally invasive procedures to address hip pain, improving mobility and quality of life without major surgery.

Plantar Fasciitis

Effective treatments for heel pain caused by plantar fasciitis, including targeted injections and therapies.

Shoulder Pain

Image-guided interventions for shoulder pain, including rotator cuff issues and arthritis-related conditions.

Neovascularization

Advanced procedures to promote new blood vessel growth, improving circulation and tissue healing.

Benign prostatic hyperplasia

Minimally invasive treatments for enlarged prostate, reducing symptoms and improving quality of life.

Uterine Fibroid

Non-surgical uterine fibroid embolization to reduce symptoms and improve quality of life.
