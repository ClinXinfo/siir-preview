Leading Expert in Interventional Radiology

Dr. Howard Lee is a highly respected Consultant Interventional Radiologist, known for his specialized skills in both vascular and nonvascular procedures. With an unwavering commitment to enhancing patient care through cutting-edge, minimally invasive techniques, Dr. Lee has established himself as a prominent authority in his field.

Internationally Recognized Expertise

Dr. Lee's distinguished career includes comprehensive training and esteemed positions at globally recognized institutions. After completing his radiology residency and fellowship in interventional radiology at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital, a premier tertiary care center in South Korea, he was appointed attending physician and assistant professor at Seoul National University Hospital. Following this, he joined Christchurch Hospital as a consultant in 2019. Dr. Lee further expanded his clinical expertise and academic contributions as an assistant professor at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center in the United States before returning to Christchurch in 2024.

Patient-Centered and Results-Driven Care

Dedicated to patient-centered care, Dr. Lee leverages the latest minimally invasive techniques and devices to achieve excellent outcomes. He offers personalized consultations and treatments at St. George's Hospital and also practices within the Interventional Radiology department at Christchurch Hospital. Through close collaboration with his medical colleagues, Dr. Lee ensures that each patient receives the highest standards of care, fostering optimal patient experiences.

Comprehensive Expertise in Specialized Procedures

Dr. Lee offers a broad range of specialized interventions, including:

  • Musculoskeletal Pain Management through Transarterial Microembolization (TAME) for chronic pain, including:
  • - Genicular Artery Embolization for knee pain
  • - Shoulder Tension & Adhesive Capsulitis, shoulder pain
  • - Elbow Pain (tennis & Golf elbow) Embolization
  • - Plantar Fasciitis Embolization
  • - Hip pain embolization for Greater Trochanteric Pain Syndrome (GTPS), hip OA
  • - Achilles Tendinitis Embolization
  • Liver Cancer Treatments: Transarterial Chemoembolization (TACE) and Transarterial Radioembolization (TARE; Y-90) for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and Liver metastasis
  • Ablative Procedures for Tumor Treatment in the kidney and liver
  • - Microwave Ablation, Cryoablation, IRE, ECT
  • Pelvic and Urinary Conditions: Ovarian Vein Embolization for pelvic congestion syndrome, Uterine Fibroid Embolization for myoma, Catheter directed sclerotherapy for ovarian endometrioma
  • Prostate Embolization: Prostate Artery Embolization for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH)
  • Hemorrhoid Embolization: Rectal Artery Embolization for Hemorrhoids
  • Peripheral Arterial Disease and Aortic Aneurysmal Disease Treatments
  • Portacath Insertions for patients requiring long-term intravenous therapy
